# Test Coverage & Quality Gates

## The 80% Rule

ArchStandards enforces a minimum coverage policy across business services and controllers:

- **Target Coverage**: 80% line and branch coverage
- Enforced by rule **`TEST-003`**

## Why Coverage Matters

Coverage thresholds prevent the gradual erosion of test quality as codebases scale. A high test ratio ensures that refactoring can be undertaken with high confidence.
